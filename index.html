How it works

  • One quiz a week in form time: 30 questions. Twenty are on GCSE subjects, five on sport and five on pop culture.
  • Your form answers each question together, with 30 seconds to decide. 1 point per correct answer.
  • Weekly scores add up over the term. The highlighted score in each column is that week's top score.
  • Missed a quiz? That week counts as 0, so make sure your form plays every week.
